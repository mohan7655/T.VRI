#### 2. Declension of masculine nouns ending in _-a_ (contd.)
<Divider />

_Accusative case_ - The case ending _-ṃ_ is added to the nominal base to form the accusative singular number. The case ending _-e_ is added to the nominal base to form the accusative case plural number. A noun thus inflected is used as the object of a sentence. The goal of motion is also expressed by the accusative case.

{" "}

<br /> <br />

<Verses>
<Box sx={{width:"50%", borderRight:{md:1}}}>
**Singular**<br/>

1. nara + ṃ = naraṃ<br/>
2. mātula + ṃ = mātulaṃ<br/>
3. kassaka + ṃ = kassakaṃ<br/>

</Box>
<Box sx={{width:"50%"}}>
**Plural**<br/>

nara + e = nare<br/>
mātula + e = mātule<br/>
kassaka + e = kassake<br/>

</Box>
</Verses>

#### 3. Examples in sentence formation
<Divider />

**Singular**<br/>

1. _Putto naraṃ passati_ - The son sees the man.<br/>
2. _Brāhmaṇo mātulaṃ rakkhati_ -The brahmin protects the uncle.<br/>
3. _Vāṇijo kassakaṃ paharati_ - The merchant hits the farmer.<br/>

{" "}

<br /> <br />
**Plural**
<br />

1. _Puttā nare passanti_ - Sons see men.<br/>
2. _Brāhmaṇā mātule rakkhanti_ - Brahmins protect uncles.<br/>
3. _Vāṇijā kassake paharanti_ - Merchants hit farmers.<br/>
