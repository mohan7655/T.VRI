#### 1. The Gerund, the Absolutive or the Indeclinable Participle

<Divider />

The suffix _-tvā_ is added to the root of the verb or verbal base\* with or sometimes without the connecting vowel _-i-_ to form the gerund, absolutive or the indeclinable participle.<br/>

_pac + i + tvā = pacitvā_ = having cooked<br/>
_khād + i + tvā = khāditvā_ = having eaten<br/>
_gam + tvā = gantvā_ = having gone<br/>
_han + tvā = hantvā_ = having killed<br/>

The suffix _-ya_ is sometimes added to roots with a prefix.<br/>

_ā + gam + ya = āgamma_(with assimilation)= having come<br/>
_ā + dā + ya = ādāya_ = having taken<br/>
_ā + ruh + ya = āruyha_(with metathesis) = having climbed<br/>
_ava + ruh + ya = oruyha_ (with metathesis) = having descended<br/>

#### 2. Attention may be paid to the following forms:
<Divider />

- bhuñjati - bhuñjitvā, bhutvā<br/>
- āgacchati - āgantvā, āgamma<br/>
- hanati - hanitvā, hantvā<br/>
- dadāti - daditvā, datvā<br/>
- nahāyati - nahāyitvā, nahātvā<br/>
- tiṭṭhati - ṭhatvā<br/>
- nikkhamati - nikkhamitvā, nikkhamma<br/>
- pajahati - pajahitvā, pahāya<br/>
- passati - passitvā; but disvā is more commonly used from the root _d¥œ_ to see,instead of passitvā.<br/>
- uṭṭhahati - uṭṭhahitvā, uṭṭhāya<br/>

<sup>
  The root is the simplest element of a verb without prefixes, suffixes or
  terminations. These are normally given in Sanskrit in grammars by Western
  scholars. The base is formed by adding a suffix to the root before a
  termination.
  <br />
  e.g.
  <br /> _pac_ is the root; _paca_ is the base
  <br /> _khād_ is the root; _khāda_ is the base
  <br /> _bhuj_ is the root; _bhuñja_ is the base
  <br /> _gam_ is the root; _gaccha_ is the base
</sup>

#### 3. Examples in sentence formation
<Divider />

1. _Kassako khettamhā āgantvā bhattaṃ bhuñjati._<br/>
   The farmer, having come from the field, eats rice.
2. _Vānarā rukkhaṃ āruyha phalāni khādanti._<br/>
   Having climbed the tree, monkeys eat fruits.
3. _Dārako bhattaṃ yācitvā rodati._<br/>
   Having asked for rice, the child cries.
4. _Samaṇo Buddhaṃ passitvā vandati._<br/>
   Having seen the Buddha, the recluse worships (him).
