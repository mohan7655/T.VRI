#### 2. Declension of masculine nouns ending in _-a_ (contd.)

<Divider />

_Locative case_ - Case endings _-e_ / _-mhi_ / _-smiṃ_ are added to the nominal base to form the locative singular.

The case ending _-esu_ is added to form the locative plural.

<br />
<br />
##### Singular

1. nara + e / mhi / smiṃ = nare, naramhi, narasmiṃ (in / on / at the man)
2. mātula + e / mhi / smiṃ = mātule, mātulamhi, mātulasmiṃ (in / on / at the uncle)
3. kassaka + e / mhi / smiṃ = kassake, kassakamhi, kassakasmiṃ (in / on / at the farmer)

<br />
<br />
##### Plural

1. nara + esu = naresu (in / on / at men)
2. mātula + esu = mātulesu (in / on / at uncles)
3. kassaka + esu = kassakesu (in / on / at farmers)

<br />
<br />
#### 3. Examples in sentence formation

<Divider />

##### Singular

1. _Sappo narasmiṃ patati._ The snake falls on the man.
2. _Putto mātulamhi pasīdati._ The son is pleased with the uncle.
3. _Vāṇijo kassakasmiṃ pasīdati._ The merchant is pleased with the farmer.

<br />
<br />
##### Plural

1. _Sappā naresu patanti._ Snakes fall on men.
2. _Puttā mātulesu pasīdanti._ Sons are pleased with their uncles.
3. _Vāṇijā kassakesu pasīdanti._ Merchants are pleased with farmers.
