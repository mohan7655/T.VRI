#### 1. Declension of masculine nouns ending in _-a_ (contd.)

<Divider />

_Genitive case_ - The inflections of the genitive case are very similar to those of the dative case.

The case ending _-ssa_ is added to the nominal base to form the genitive singular.

The case ending _-ānaṃ_ is added to form the genitive plural.

<br />
<br />
##### Singular

1. nara + ssa = narassa (of the man)
2. mātula + ssa = mātulassa (of the uncle)
3. kassaka + ssa = kassakassa (of the farmer)

<br />
<br />
##### Plural

1. nara + ānaṃ = narānaṃ (of the men)
2. mātula + ānaṃ = mātulānaṃ (of the uncles)
3. kassaka + ānaṃ = kassakānaṃ (of the farmers)

<br />
<br />
#### 2. Examples in sentence formation
<Divider />

##### Singular

1. _Narassa putto bhattaṃ yācati._ - The man's son asks for rice.
2. _Mātulassa sahāyako rathaṃ āharati._ The uncle's friend brings the vehicle.
3. _Kassakassa sūkaro dīpaṃ dhāvati._ The farmer's pig runs to the island.

<br />
<br />
##### Plural

1. _Narānaṃ puttā bhattaṃ yācanti._ - Sons of the men ask for rice.
2. _Mātulānaṃ sahāyakā rathe āharanti._ Uncles' friends bring vehicles.
3. _Kassakānaṃ sūkarā dīpe dhāvanti._ Farmers' pigs run to the islands.
